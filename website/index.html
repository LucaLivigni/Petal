Drag a file. Tap a key. Drop it on a wedge.

Petal is a menu bar utility for Mac that converts, compresses and sends files from a wheel that opens right under your cursor. Everything runs on your Mac.

A simulated Mac with sample files. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is converted for real, but it behaves like the app: drag, tap a key, drop.

Try this

Your client wants the holiday photo as a JPG, under 2 MB.

  1. Drag holiday.heic, tap ⇧, drop it on JPG.
  2. Drag the new JPG onto the Mail window.
  3. Drag weekend.mov, tap ⌘, drop it on Compress to send it too.

Free play: every file on the desktop is draggable.

      Drag a file, tap a key to choose the wheel, then drop it on a wedge: ⇧ Convert · ⌘ Tools · ⌃ Control.
      
        
      Keyboard only: focus a file and press Enter, like Enter on a selection in the app. Arrow keys move, Enter runs, Esc closes.
      
        
      

        
      
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
      
        
      Accent: Tangerine. Petal ships with nine to choose from.

      There is nothing to open. Petal waits in the menu bar until you start dragging.
      
        
      
        
        
          
      1. 
            
        
              
            
        
            
        Drag a file

        Tap a key

      Three wheels. One for each job.
      
          
      Each wheel shows what makes sense for the file in your hand. It learns the formats you reach for most and puts them first, and you can set the order yourself.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      ⇧
      
            
      Convert
      
            
      Tap Shift
      
            
      Change a file into another format. Photos, video, audio, PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, subtitles and archives.
      
            
      JPG to PNG or WebP. MOV to MP4 or GIF. WAV to M4A. PDF to DOCX. XLSX to CSV. ZIP to TAR.GZ.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      ⌘
      
            
      Tools
      
            
      Tap Command
      
            
      Work on the file itself: shrink it, tidy it, pull something out of it. Small editor windows cover cropping a photo and trimming audio or video.
      
            
      Compress, resize, remove background, copy text from an image, rotate, flip, black and white, strip metadata, split, merge or rotate a PDF, mute video, make a GIF, extract audio, checksum, size, zip.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      ⌃
      
            
      Actions
      
            
      Tap Control
      
            
      Send the file somewhere, or keep it close at hand, without leaving what you are doing.
      
            
      AirDrop, Copy, Copy path, Move to…, Copy to…, Open in Terminal, Float to keep the file on screen, Show in Finder.
      
          
      
        
      
        
      After every job a small card appears. Drag the result straight out of it, or show it in Finder.

      Your files never leave your Mac.
      
        
      Petal does its work on the computer in front of you. There is no server on the other end.
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Nothing is uploaded
      
            
      Conversion, text recognition, background removal and every other tool run on your Mac, and they work without a network connection.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      No account
      
            
      There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. Download it, open it, drag a file.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      No analytics, no tracking
      
            
      Petal does not measure what you do or what you convert, and it does not record what you type.

      Questions
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            Does Petal work offline?
            
      Yes. Everything happens on your Mac, so no connection is needed. Petal does not make network connections.
      
          
      
          
      
            What about slides, Pages documents and old Excel files?
            
      PPT, PPTX and Keynote files can be turned into PDF, JPG or PNG, and Pages documents and old XLS spreadsheets can be read, by asking Apple’s free Keynote, Pages and Numbers apps to do the work. Install the ones you need.
      
          
      
          
      
            What do I need to grant?
            
      Nothing special. Petal watches mouse drags and the modifier keys, which macOS allows without a permission prompt, and it never records typing. If you use Keynote, Pages, Numbers or Open in Terminal, macOS may ask once whether Petal can control that app.
      
          
      
          
      
            How does the wheel learn my order?
            
      Petal notices which formats and tools you pick for each kind of file and moves the ones you use most to the front of the wheel. You can also arrange the order yourself.
      
          
      
          
      
            Where do my results go?
            
      Next to the original, as a new copy, unless you choose another folder. Petal never overwrites a file; if the name is taken it adds a number.
      
          
      
          
      
            Can I change the keys?
            
      Yes. Shift, Command and Control are the defaults for Convert, Tools and Actions, and you can assign others. The size of the wheel (S or M) and the accent colour are settings too.
