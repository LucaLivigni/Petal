Privacy

Your files never leave your Mac. Petal has no server, no account and no analytics.

The app

Petal does its work on your computer. When you drop a file on a wedge, the conversion or tool runs locally, and the result is saved next to the original, or in a folder you chose.

  • Nothing is uploaded. Your files, their names and their contents are never sent anywhere. Petal makes no network connections, so it also works offline.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No analytics and no tracking. Petal does not measure how you use it and does not collect identifiers.
  • No keystroke recording. To know when you tap a key during a drag, Petal reads the state of the modifier keys (Shift, Command, Control). It never records what you type.

What stays on your Mac

Petal keeps its own settings on your Mac: the keys you picked, the accent colour, the wheel size, the order of formats and which ones you use most. The usage counts that let the wheel put your favourite formats first never leave the device.

Other apps Petal can use

Slides, Pages documents and old Excel files are converted by asking Apple’s Keynote, Pages and Numbers, if you have them installed. Petal does this through macOS, and macOS asks you once whether to allow it. Open in Terminal works the same way. Those apps are governed by Apple’s own privacy policies, and the files stay on your Mac.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It runs no analytics, sets no cookies and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other sites. The server that hosts the files may keep ordinary access logs, as web servers usually do.

Applies to Petal 0.1.0.
